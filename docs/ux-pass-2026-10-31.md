## The overview's order (Karthik's change)

See DECISIONS.md, "The overview's order". In short: New page first, then most recently changed; page numbers,
⌘[ / ⌘] and "Page 9 of 18" keep the notebook's order; the highlight starts on the page you came from (G, G
unchanged); arrows follow the tiles as shown; transitions use the tile's own position; search results come in
tile order; ink, the eraser, scratch-out, undo, redo and Clear Page count as a change, a view change and a
blank eraser swipe don't; created date as fallback, undated pages last. `checks/galleryorder.sh` fails 11 of
17 on 9594d74.

## Not fixed, and why

- **A stray first stroke after entering draw mode** (friction audit #1): 25 of 135 first strokes were undone
  before the next (19%). The fix (ignore a finger already down at entry) is in the input path two days before
  release, and it could drop real first strokes. Needs Karthik's call and a replay check.
- **Panels by VoiceOver or keyboard:** sizes, colours, paper and line width open with Show Menu but their
  swatches aren't elements. 1–5 cover size; the rest needs the pointer.
- **⌘Z while typing in the overview's search** undoes ink, not the typing.
- **Reduce Motion** changes only the overview: the next-line move and the resume glide still animate (both
  signed off; a jump could lose your place).
- **The smallest window's overview** has one column.
- **The pointer resting over the overview** moves the highlight there when it opens (by design; it made the
  robot screenshots' highlight land on whatever tile was under the pointer).
- **Not verified here:** real full screen (it would take over the screen), an external display (none attached),
  a fresh macOS account (no admin password; a quarantined DMG copy, an empty data folder and a fresh defaults
  domain stood in), and the GitHub release page (the repo isn't visible to this machine's token).
