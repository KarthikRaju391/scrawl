Write and sketch with a finger on your Mac's trackpad: Keep-style tools, infinite pages, and a writing
mode that takes you to the next line when you reach the end of one.

**Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later and a trackpad.** Built for Apple silicon and Intel (Intel is untested).

## Install

1. Download `Scrawl-0.1.6.dmg` below, open it, and drag **Scrawl** into **Applications** (replace the old copy).
2. This build isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS asks before the first open:
   - **macOS 14:** Control-click the app in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
   - **macOS 15 and later:** open it once, then go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security**,
     click **Open Anyway** next to "Scrawl was blocked", and confirm.
3. Press **⌃⌥D** to start drawing and **Esc** to get the pointer back.

## What's new in 0.1.6: pictures in your notebook

- Paste an image (⌘V), drag one onto the page, or choose **File › Insert Image…** (⇧⌘I). It lands on the page
  you're on, under your writing, ready to place.
- With the Select tool, drag it to move it and drag a corner to resize it (hold Shift to change its proportions).
  Delete it with ⌫, bring it forward or send it back with ⌥⌘] and ⌥⌘[, or copy it with ⌘C. Every change can be
  undone.
- Write over it with any tool. Scratch-out and the eraser only ever touch your writing.
- **File › New Page from Image…** (⇧⌘N) makes a new page with the picture across it. The page header says where it
  came from.
- PNG, JPEG, HEIC, TIFF, GIF, screenshots, and PDFs (the first page).
- Pictures are kept at their full resolution, unchanged, and stay sharp as you zoom in. Exports, Copy as Image and
  the pages overview include them.
- Your daily backups keep their pictures.
- Scrawl 0.1.5 and older can still open your notebook; they show your writing without the pictures. If you save in
  an older version, those pages lose their pictures (the picture files stay next to the notebook).

Scrawl sends anonymous usage counts (never your ink, pages or text); **Settings › Share anonymous usage counts**
turns them off. [What's counted](https://github.com/KarthikRaju391/scrawl/blob/main/docs/analytics.md).
